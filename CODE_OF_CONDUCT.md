# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We want this to be a welcoming space for teachers, students and developers of every
background and level of experience. In issues, pull requests and any other project space:

- be kind, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers and students;
- assume good intent, and critique ideas rather than people;
- respect different teaching contexts, languages and abilities;
- do not harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately, and do not share others'
  private information.

If someone's behaviour concerns you, email the maintainer at rakesh.sengupta@krea.edu.in.
Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, or block participants, that break this code.
