Neural Signalling Labs

Three browser labs for teaching how neurons signal, written for psychology students. Students change a model neuron, measure what happens, and replicate classic findings, beside the slides or in an exam.

  • Runs on phones and laptops
  • Nothing to install
  • Nothing is saved or sent
  • Light and dark themes
  • Free and open source

Spike Lab

The action potential (Hodgkin–Huxley model)

  1. Find the threshold
  2. Watch the sodium and potassium channels; block them
  3. The refractory period
  4. Rate coding
Open Spike Lab Booklet · Slides

Relay Lab

Conduction, synapses and receptors

  1. Race spikes along bare and myelinated axons
  2. Order the steps of a synapse; add up inputs
  3. Agonists, antagonists and reuptake blockers
Open Relay Lab Booklet · Slides · passwords on the slides

Prediction Lab

Replicating Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997)

  1. Be the monkey: learn when a reward comes
  2. Record a dopamine neuron (Fig. 1)
  3. Watch prediction errors move (Fig. 3)
  4. Test the theory, including where it fails
Open Prediction Lab Question booklet · start password: juice

For instructors

Each lab comes with a student booklet; Spike Lab and Relay Lab also have slide decks (LaTeX Beamer). The instructor guide gives learning objectives, timings and how to adapt the labs; the model notes say exactly what each simulation does and where it simplifies.
